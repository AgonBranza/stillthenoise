what it is

take the lyrics,
make them yours.

stillthenoise.com is not a website that promotes an album. it is the album, taken apart. the record's own language became six interactive tools, and what people make inside them collects in a shared, public archive.

it started because when the early demos were played to friends, before the melody, before anything else, they went straight for the words. some of them cried. that reaction shaped the whole project.

so instead of a normal rollout, the words were handed over as raw material. nobody is asked to admire the songs. they are given the language to rearrange until it says something true about their own life. the triptych and magnetic poetry pieces can be printed, which turns a private, half anonymous act of writing with someone else's words into an object a person can hold.

the silent rooms

one interactive lyric experience per song. the site plays no audio: you bring the music, and the room responds around it.

the silent rooms were built as an alternative to the music video, meant to bring people closer to the words while they are actually listening, the way a physical cd booklet once did. because the site is silent by design, the listener supplies half the work.

the community

the holding room

shared fragments of what we carry

everything made in the tools can be left in a public, moderated archive: featured selections, a wall of unsent letters, and an open feed. people post under a name, an instagram handle, or anonymously.

it is the part that turns a solo record into a collective one. strangers leaving fragments of what they are holding, all built from the same set of words.

who made it

agon branza is the artist name of gonçalo abrantes, a lisbon born, london based singer songwriter, composer and producer. his work draws from classical music, confessional songwriting and avant garde electronic production, shaped by conservatoire training in piano and theory and by his studies in cinema and music for film.

as a composer, working under his given name, his scoring spans advertising and award winning independent film, including a microsoft campaign that won his team a silver lion at cannes. his piano cycle inner anthems has been streamed more than 40,000 times and featured across three of nils frahm's piano day playlists.

he has performed and recorded for alanis morissette, patrick watson, imogen heap, jools holland, nitin sawhney, emeli sandé, manu delago and the bbc orchestra, at the royal albert hall, abbey road studio one and the barbican.

the record  ·  out early 2027

eleven tracks, one loop

most breakup albums go straight for blame. this one does not. it is about accountability instead, the harder and quieter thing, written from a queer perspective, and it tries to hold both sides of a story rather than point at one.

it moves through four stages, questioning, accountability, acceptance and impermanence, and it opens and closes on the same wall of noise. the first time that noise is a conflict already happening. by the end it is release. it never fully resolves, because these things do not.
